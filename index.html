G Family Finance

A private, self-hosted personal finance dashboard used only by the G household to view our own accounts.

What it is

G Family Finance is a personal tool, not a commercial product or service. It runs on a single home computer and is not offered to the public. Its only users are members of the G household, viewing accounts they own.

What it does

  • Shows balances for our own checking, savings, credit card and investment accounts in one place.
  • Tracks net worth over time.
  • Categorizes transactions and compares spending to a monthly household budget.
  • Shows investment holdings, cost basis and gains.

How accounts are connected

Accounts are connected through Plaid. We sign in to our own financial institutions through Plaid Link. The app never sees or stores our bank usernames or passwords. It receives read-only account, balance, transaction and investment data.

How data is handled

  • All data is stored locally on the household computer, not on any server or cloud service.
  • Plaid access tokens are encrypted at rest. The encryption keys are tied to the local Windows user account.
  • The app accepts connections only from the computer it runs on. It is not reachable from the internet or the local network.
  • Data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.
