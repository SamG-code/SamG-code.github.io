Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

G Family Finance ("the app") is a private, self-hosted dashboard used only by members of the G household to view financial accounts they own. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data we collect

With the account holder's consent, given when they sign in through Plaid Link, the app receives the following from Plaid:

  • Account names, types, masked account numbers and balances
  • Transaction history (date, amount, merchant, category)
  • Investment holdings, securities and investment transactions

The app never receives or stores online banking usernames or passwords. Those are entered directly with Plaid or the financial institution.

How data is used

Data is used only to show the household its own balances, net worth, spending, budgets and investments. It is not used for advertising, marketing, credit decisions, or any other purpose.

Where data is stored

  • All data is stored in a local database on the household's own computer. There is no server, cloud storage, or remote backup operated by the app.
  • Plaid access tokens are encrypted at rest using Windows Data Protection (DPAPI), so they can only be decrypted by the household's Windows user account on that computer.
  • The app accepts connections only from the computer it runs on. It cannot be reached from the internet or the local network.

Sharing

Data is never sold, rented, or shared with any third party. The only outside service involved is Plaid, which provides the connection to financial institutions under its own End User Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept on the household computer until the household deletes it. Removing a connected institution in the app revokes its access through Plaid. Deleting the app's local data folder removes all stored data. Account holders can also manage or revoke Plaid's access at my.plaid.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
